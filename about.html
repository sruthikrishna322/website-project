Our Story

Our Story
Sweet Crumbs began in 2016 when a mother-daughter duo converted their love of baking into a neighborhood bakery on Baker Street. What started as a few dozen loaves and cookies every morning quickly grew into a community favorite — known for attention to detail, seasonal flavors, and celebration cakes that make memories.

Sweet Crumbs Bakery started as a small kitchen dream and has grown into a community favorite. Our bakers combine traditional recipes with a modern twist, ensuring every treat is as delightful as it is delicious.

Our Mission
To make every moment sweeter by baking high-quality, thoughtfully created pastries and cakes using fresh, locally sourced ingredients whenever possible. We care about flavor, texture, and presentation — and about making our customers smile.

What We Bake
From crusty breads and morning croissants to delicate pastries, cupcakes, and custom tiered cakes, our menu blends classic recipes with modern flavors. Popular items include our Chocolate Dual Cake, Rainbow Pastries, and Red Velvet Cookies.

We believe in using only the finest ingredients — no shortcuts, just love, care, and passion in every bake.
